How it works

Three calm steps to a safe return

No accounts, no spam, no public phone numbers. Just a clear path from “missing” to “mine again”.

  1. 01

    Look for a match

    Switch between lost and found, then narrow the board by category, campus location or keyword.

  2. 02

    Share a few details

    Describe the item, where it was lost or found, and add an optional photo. Contact details are never listed publicly.

  3. 03

    Arrange a safe return

    Submit a claim with a detail only the owner would know, then meet in a busy, well-lit campus spot.

Privacy & safety

Care for the person, not just the item

A found item is someone’s bad day. These habits keep every handover kind and safe.

Keep personal details private

Campus emails are used only to match a report with its claims. Never post phone numbers, room numbers or ID photos on a listing.

Confirm it belongs to you

Ask claimants for a detail nobody could guess — a scratch, a sticker, what is inside the wallet. Scratched serial numbers are perfect.

Meet somewhere safe

Choose the library entrance, Uni Mall or the Student Welfare office during daylight hours. Bring a friend for high-value items.

What makes a good report

  • Name the item the way a friend would describe it.
  • Pick the closest campus location, then add the exact spot.
  • Mention one detail a genuine owner would recognise.
  • Add a photo with a plain background if you can.

Privacy reminder

Never include ID numbers, bank details or photos of documents. This board is a class demonstration and LocalStorage is not encrypted.
